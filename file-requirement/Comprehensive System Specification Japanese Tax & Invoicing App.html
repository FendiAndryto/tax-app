Comprehensive System Specification: Japanese Tax & Invoicing App (MVP)

Architecture Stack: React Native (Expo & Expo Web) + PHP (Laravel REST API) + MySQL

Core Architectural Directive:
The application provides a simplified single-entry frontend designed for Japanese freelancers, independent contractors, and partner agency/hospitality staff. In the background, the engine automates full double-entry bookkeeping (複式簿記) complying with Japanese National Tax Agency standards (Blue Return 650,000 JPY deduction, Qualified Invoice System, and Article 204 withholding rules).

3. Specialized Japanese Tax Engine Logic

A. Partner Agency / Hospitality Per-Day Withholding Tax (Article 204)

For registered performers and hospitality staff, withholding tax applies an allowance of 5,000 JPY per day worked:

$deductible_base = 5000 * $attendance_days;
if ($gross_revenue > $deductible_base) {
    $withholding_tax = floor(($gross_revenue - $deductible_base) * 0.1021);
} else {
    $withholding_tax = 0;
}

B. Qualified Invoice Tax & Rounding Rules

Under Japanese Invoice regulations, tax MUST NOT be calculated and rounded per item line. Pre-tax amounts are grouped by tax rate (10% and 8%), and rounding occurs exactly once per tax bracket.

  • Configurable rounding methods: Floor (切り捨て), Ceil (切り上げ), or Round (四捨五入).
  • Reverse Calculation Mode: For total-inclusive payments (e.g., ¥39,000 rounded payout), back-calculate: 
    $pre_tax_total = floor($inclusive_grand_total / 1.1);
$consumption_tax = $inclusive_grand_total - $pre_tax_total;

4. Sub-Systems & Advanced Modules

A. Rule-Based Auto-Categorization Engine

Enables automatic classification of imported or synchronized records:

  • Matching Conditions: Transaction description substring (e.g., contains "Seven-Eleven") OR specific institution ID.
  • Triggered Actions: Set Status (Business / Private), Assign Category ID, Apply default Business Split Ratio (0-100%), and execute by Priority order.

B. Fixed Asset Ledger & Depreciation Engine (固定資産台帳)

Manages capital purchases exceeding 100,000 JPY:

  • Properties: Asset Name, Purchase Date, In-service Date, Acquisition Cost, Quantity, Useful Life (Statutory Years), Business Usage Ratio.
  • Depreciation Methods:
    • Straight-Line (定額法): Standard equal annual depreciation.
    • Declining-Balance (定率法): Accelerated depreciation.
    • Small-Sum Immediate (少額減価償却資産特例): Under 300,000 JPY fully expensed in the current year.
    • Lump-Sum (一括償却): Equal split over 3 consecutive years.

C. Qualified Invoice & Quotation Generator

  • Captures: Qualified Issuer Number (T + 13 digits), Client Name, Invoice Date, Due Date, Itemized Lines, Bank Settlement Details.
  • Generates official PDF complying with National Tax Agency Qualified Invoice formatting.

D. Data Import & Export Modules

  • Import: Accepts CSV files exported from major Japanese tax software (freee, MoneyForward, Yayoi, and PayPay).
  • Export: Generates Journal Ledger (仕訳帳) and General Ledger (総勘定元帳) in CSV formats (supporting both UTF-8 and Shift_JIS).

E. AI Consultation Assistant (スナッピー)

  • In-app floating AI chatbot.
  • Provides real-time answers regarding deductible criteria, tax filing deadlines, and categorization recommendations.
